# TutorCircle
*Peer Tutoring with Group Sessions and Learning Follow-Up*

The Vision: Build a tutoring platform where students find suitable peer tutors, join individual
or small-group sessions, practise what they learn, and receive follow-up feedback. Imagine
three students struggling with UML sequence diagrams: they find a tutor, complete a short
preparation quiz, attend a group session, submit an exercise, and see which topics still need
work. TutorCircle connects booking with a continuing learning journey.

Problem Being Solved: Tutoring arrangements often depend on informal messages, uncertain
availability, and disconnected learning materials. Students may attend a session without clear
goals, while tutors have little information about prior difficulties or subsequent progress. Group
bookings also introduce capacity, cancellation, and waiting-list problems. TutorCircle
organizes the complete experience for learners, tutors, and a coordinator.
